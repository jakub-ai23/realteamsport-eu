The brand

A travel agency that also runs its own events.

Most travel agencies sell other people's trips. We wanted to know what it feels like on the other side of the desk, so we built a sport brand of our own and ran it for five years.

Future Roundnet is that brand. It is owned and operated by REAL TEAM, s.r.o. — same company, same licence, same insurance. Its camp is FRESH: the world's biggest roundnet training camp, five editions in Mallorca, players from 27 countries.

Running it taught us where group travel actually breaks. Rooming lists that change on the way to the airport. Payments arriving from seven countries in four currencies. A hotel that has never hosted the sport before. Every one of those lessons went straight into how we handle other people's groups.

That is the point of this page. Future Roundnet is not a side project we mention for colour. It is the proof that we have stood where our clients stand.

The camp, in short.

A week of coaching
