Group travel

You bring the group. We handle the rest.

Training camps, club trips and team weeks for sport communities, organised end to end by a licensed travel agency.

Tell us about your group

Who this is for

Whatever the sport, the admin is the same.

A roundnet community, a diving community, a handball club, a training group that wants a week somewhere warm. The sport decides what you do when you get there. It has almost no bearing on what it takes to get fifty people there in the first place.

That part is the same every time, and it is the part we do.

The problem

Someone in your club is doing this in a spreadsheet right now.

Every club trip follows the same pattern. One person volunteers. They start a spreadsheet. Then come the rooming preferences, the two people who arrive a day late, the dietary requirements, the person who pays in three instalments, the transfer that has to be split across two buses, and the hotel that answers every email with a new person on copy.

By the time the group lands, the volunteer has spent forty hours on admin and is too tired to enjoy the trip they organised.

That is the job we take off you.

What we handle

The unglamorous half.

You keep control of the sport. We take everything that sits around it.

01

Hotel and negotiation

Finding the right venue for a group your size, negotiating the rate, holding the rooms, and dealing with the hotel so your volunteer does not have to.

02

Rooming and payments

Rooming lists, split arrival and departure dates, individual payments and the running total. Handled as a process, not as a favour.

03

Transfers and logistics

Airport transfers, ground transport, training facilities and whatever the week needs on top. One point of contact throughout.

Why us

We run our own events, so we know where it breaks.

Most travel agencies sell group trips. Fewer of them organise their own events with a hundred participants and then live with the consequences for a week.

Future Roundnet is our own brand and FRESH is its camp, run five times. Every awkward part of group travel has already gone wrong on us at least once: the arrival that shifts by a day, the rooming list that changes on the drive to the airport, the invoice that has to be split eleven ways. We built the way we work around those failures.

We are also a properly licensed travel agency, which matters more than it sounds. A CK licence means insolvency protection is in place and your group's money is covered by law rather than by trust.
