Key rights under Directive (EU) 2015/2302

Where the services you book from us form a package within the meaning of the Directive, you have all the rights that apply to packages. In summary:

  • You will receive all essential information about the package before you conclude the contract.
  • There is always at least one trader liable for the proper performance of all the services included in the contract.
  • You are given an emergency telephone number or the details of a contact point through which you can reach the organiser.
  • You may transfer the package to another person, on reasonable notice and possibly subject to additional costs.
  • The price may only be increased in specific cases, no later than 20 days before the start of the package, and only if the contract expressly reserves that possibility. If the increase exceeds 8% of the price, you may terminate the contract. Where the organiser reserves the right to increase the price, you are equally entitled to a price reduction if the relevant costs fall.
  • You may terminate the contract without paying a termination fee and receive a full refund if any essential element of the package other than the price is changed significantly.
  • You may terminate the contract without paying a termination fee before the start of the package in the event of unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances at the destination which significantly affect the performance of the package.
  • You may terminate the contract at any time before the start of the package against payment of an appropriate and justifiable termination fee.
  • If significant elements of the package cannot be provided as agreed, suitable alternative arrangements must be offered at no extra cost. You may terminate the contract without a termination fee where services are not performed in accordance with the contract and this substantially affects the performance of the package.
  • You are entitled to a price reduction, compensation for damages, or both, where the travel services are not performed or are improperly performed.
  • The organiser must provide assistance if you are in difficulty.
  • If the organiser becomes insolvent, payments are refunded. Where insolvency occurs after the start of the package and transport is included, repatriation is secured.

The Slovak legislation transposing the Directive is Act No. 170/2018 Coll., available at slov-lex.sk.
