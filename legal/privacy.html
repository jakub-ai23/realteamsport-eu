Privacy

We have not appointed a data protection officer, as we are not required to under Article 37 GDPR.

What this website does not do

  • It sets no cookies of any kind.
  • It runs no analytics and no tracking scripts.
  • It loads no fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers. Our typefaces are served from our own domain precisely so that your IP address is never passed to a font CDN.
  • It has no contact form, no newsletter sign-up and no account system.
  • It embeds no social media widgets, no video players and no maps.

Because of this, no consent banner is necessary. There is nothing to consent to.

Server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Like any web server, GitHub's infrastructure processes technical connection data when a page is requested. This can include your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, the referring page and your browser and operating system version.

We do not have access to these logs, do not receive them and cannot link them to a person. GitHub processes them to deliver the site and to protect its infrastructure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a functioning, secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

This involves a transfer of data to a processor in the United States. GitHub is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Details of GitHub's own processing are set out in the GitHub Privacy Statement at docs.github.com.

If you email us

When you write to us, we process your email address, your name if you give it, and the content of your message, for the purpose of answering you. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message relates to a contract or a pre-contractual enquiry, and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise.

Business correspondence is retained for as long as our commercial and tax record-keeping obligations under Slovak law require. Everything else is deleted once the matter is closed and there is no reason to keep it.

Bookings and events

This website does not take bookings. Where you register for one of our events or trips, the personal data required for that booking is collected on the relevant booking platform, and you are informed there about how it is handled. For FRESH, that is futureroundnet.com.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to request access to your personal data, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data erased, to have processing restricted, to data portability, and to object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time with effect for the future.

To exercise any of these rights, write to jakub@realteamsport.eu.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Slovakia this is the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky), Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava — dataprotection.gov.sk.

Changes

If we add anything to this site that processes data differently — analytics, a contact form, an embedded map — this page is updated before that feature goes live, not after.

Draft status. This policy describes the site exactly as built today: no cookies, no analytics, self-hosted fonts. If analytics are added later, it must be rewritten and a consent mechanism considered. Review by the company's accountant or a Slovak lawyer is recommended before publication.
