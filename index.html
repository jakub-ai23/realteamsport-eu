Who we are

The third chapter of a company that never stopped working.

REAL TEAM was founded in Slovakia in 1994 and has held a CK travel agency licence ever since. For the first two decades it ran school ski trips, handball tournaments and sport travel across Central Europe. Unglamorous, well-organised work, repeated year after year for people who came back.

I took the company over from my father in 2023. Same licence, same registration number, same idea. What changed is where we go and who we take.

My own detour got me here: I studied in Munich and in Australia, worked in tourism across Europe, spent time in more than fifty countries, and played floorball for Slovakia. Somewhere in there I started organising trips for other people and never really stopped.

A thirty-year-old licence is not a marketing line. It means someone kept the paperwork current, the insurance paid and the promises kept, in years when nobody was watching.

The honest part

Some of it did not work.

I have organised events that sold out and events that never happened. I built a coaching app that went nowhere. I ran a podcast for two years and stopped when I ran out of things worth asking.

That is on the company website on purpose. If you are thinking about handing us your group, your event or your hotel's rooms, you should know what you are dealing with: a small company that keeps trying things, gets most of them right, and says so plainly when it does not.
