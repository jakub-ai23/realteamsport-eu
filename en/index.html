Frequently asked questions

Who do you organise trips for?

For clubs, teams and sport communities. Tell us the dates, how many people and what you need, and we put together an offer and arrange the stay.

What do you arrange?

Accommodation and meals, the rooming list and individual payments with a clear view of who has paid, airport transfers and local transport, training facilities and excursions during the stay.

Which destinations do you travel to?

Our main destinations are Mallorca and Albania. We can also arrange a stay in another destination you choose.

Are you a licensed travel agency?

Yes. REAL TEAM, s.r.o., company ID 31 369 049, registered at Pri kríži 18, 841 02 Bratislava, Slovakia, has held a travel agency licence since 1994.

We hold insolvency insurance for travel agencies under Slovak Act No. 170/2018 Coll. with Union poisťovňa, a.s. The policy number and the scope of cover are in the legal information.

What is FRESH?
