Protection against insolvency

The company holds travel agency insolvency insurance under Act No. 170/2018 Coll. Traveller rights

Where the services form a package within the meaning of Act No. 170/2018 Coll., transposing Directive (EU) 2015/2302, travellers have all the rights that apply to packages, in particular:

  • all essential information about the package before the contract is concluded,
  • at least one trader is liable for the proper performance of all services,
  • a contact point through which the organiser can be reached,
  • the package may be transferred to another person on reasonable notice and possibly at additional cost,
  • the price may only be increased in specific cases and no later than 20 days before the start; if the increase exceeds 8% the traveller may terminate the contract,
  • the contract may be terminated without a fee, with a full refund, if an essential element other than the price is changed significantly,
  • the contract may be terminated without a fee in the event of unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances at the destination,
  • the contract may be terminated at any time before the start against an appropriate termination fee,
  • where essential services cannot be provided as agreed, suitable alternatives must be offered at no extra cost,
  • the traveller is entitled to a price reduction, compensation, or both, where services are improperly performed,
  • the organiser must provide assistance if the traveller is in difficulty,
  • on insolvency, payments are refunded and repatriation is secured where transport is included.

The text of Act No. 170/2018 Coll. is available at slov-lex.sk.

Enquiry form

The form on the home page is for sending a message. We process your name, email address and the content of your message solely in order to reply and prepare an offer. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by ticking the box, and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry is pre-contractual.

Submitted data is processed on our behalf by Formspree, Inc. (USA), which delivers the form to our email address and acts as our processor. This involves a transfer to a third country; the data is processed only to deliver the message and is not retained longer than necessary for that purpose.

We do not use form data for marketing mailings without separate consent. You may withdraw consent at any time at jakub@realteamsport.eu.

If you tick the consent box, your message is also stored as a contact record with Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, 106 boulevard Haussmann, 75008 Paris, France), which acts as our processor. This is so the message remains on file after the email has been answered. We do not use it for marketing mailings without separate consent.

No data leaves this page when it loads. It is sent only when the form is submitted.
